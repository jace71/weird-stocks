Flanigan's Enterprises started in Florida in 1959 as a chain of small cocktail lounges and package liquor stores. By 1970 it had a chain of "Big Daddy's" lounges and liquor stores between Vero Beach and Homestead.

<h2>New name, old ticker</h2>
SEC records show the company was called Big Daddy's Lounges until March 1978, when it became Flanigan's Enterprises. The stock still trades as BDL. And Big Daddy never really left: the company's liquor stores still run under the names "Big Daddy's Liquors" and "Big Daddy's Wine & Liquors."

As of September 2025, the company operated 32 units, a mix of restaurants, liquor stores, combination restaurant-and-liquor-store locations and a sports bar, and it franchised 5 more.
