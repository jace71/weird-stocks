Some tickers are puns. This one is a job description. DMC Global trades as BOOM, and part of its business really does involve explosions.

<h2>Welding with a bang</h2>
DMC's NobelClad business makes explosion-welded clad metal plates. Explosion welding is a way to join metals that can't be joined with regular welding, like titanium and steel, aluminum and steel, or aluminum and copper. The cladding metals include titanium, stainless steel, copper alloys, nickel alloys, tantalum and zirconium.

<h2>And the rest of it</h2>
DMC runs two other businesses. DynaEnergetics makes perforating systems used to complete oil and gas wells and to plug and abandon them. Arcadia Products makes aluminum framing systems, windows and doors for commercial buildings and high-end homes.
