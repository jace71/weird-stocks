Brunswick's story starts with a carriage maker building a pool table. In 1845, John Moses Brunswick built a billiard table in Cincinnati, and his company went on to lead the billiards business. Today Brunswick is best known for boats and marine engines, and it has been on the New York Stock Exchange since January 1925.
