Some tickers take a minute to decode. This one is just MAMA.

The company used to be called MamaMancini's Holdings and renamed itself Mama's Creations in 2023. Its story goes back to Anna "Mama" Mancini, who moved from Bari, Italy, to Bay Ridge, Brooklyn, in 1921. Her old-world recipes were handed down to her grandson, Dan Dougherty, who founded the company.

Today it makes fresh deli-prepared foods sold in more than 12,000 grocery, mass, club and convenience stores, including beef, chicken and turkey meatballs, meat loaf, sausage dishes, and pasta and rice entrees.
