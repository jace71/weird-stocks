Most of us don't think about where cat litter comes from. Oil-Dri does, because it digs it out of the ground. The company mines clay on land it owns or leases in places like Georgia, Mississippi, Illinois and California, dries it in kilns, and sells it.

<h2>Sponges made of dirt</h2>
Oil-Dri's clay soaks things up. Its brands include Cat's Pride and Jonny Cat cat litter, Oil-Dri floor absorbents for soaking up spills, Amlan animal health and nutrition products for livestock, and Agsorb and Verge carriers for farm chemicals. It also makes private-label cat litter for other brands.

<h2>An old business</h2>
The company traces its roots to a partnership that started in 1941. It became a Delaware corporation in 1969, and its stock trades on the NYSE as ODC.
