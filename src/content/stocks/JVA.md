Coffee Holding Co. sells coffee, so its ticker is JVA. Read it out loud and you get java.

The company is based on Victory Boulevard in Staten Island. It imports raw, unroasted green coffee beans from around the world and sells them to other roasters and coffee shops. It also roasts coffee under store brands for supermarkets and under its own brand names. It was founded by Sterling Gordon, and the company says it has been family-run for three generations. Andrew Gordon is CEO, and David Gordon runs operations.
