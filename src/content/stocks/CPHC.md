You can own a slice of a horse track on Nasdaq. Canterbury Park Holding Corp. runs Canterbury Park Racetrack and Casino in Shakopee, Minnesota, about 20 miles southwest of downtown Minneapolis.

<h2>The only track of its kind in the state</h2>
Canterbury Park is the only facility in Minnesota that offers live pari-mutuel thoroughbred and quarter horse racing. People can bet on its own races during live meets and on races at out-of-state tracks all year. It also hosts "unbanked" card games, where players bet against each other instead of the house.

<h2>Racetrack plus real estate</h2>
The company also has land it doesn't need for racing. Under a project called Canterbury Commons, it is pursuing housing, offices, restaurants, a hotel, entertainment and stores around the track.
