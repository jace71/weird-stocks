Daily Journal Corporation sounds like a newspaper company, and it is one, sort of. It publishes newspapers and websites covering California and Arizona, including the Los Angeles and San Francisco Daily Journals, and it places public notice ads, like foreclosure notices.

<h2>Mostly software, actually</h2>
The bigger business is Journal Technologies, a subsidiary that sells case management software to courts, prosecutors, public defenders, probation departments and other justice agencies. In fiscal 2025, Journal Technologies brought in about 80% of the company's revenue.

<h2>The Munger connection</h2>
Daily Journal also owns a portfolio of stocks, which is unusual for a small publisher. Charlie Munger, the investor famous for his work at Berkshire Hathaway, was a director of the company for many decades and long managed those holdings. He died on November 28, 2023.

This is a fun profile, not investment advice.
