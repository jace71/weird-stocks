Petco sells food, toys and supplies for pets, so its stock ticker is exactly what you'd hope: WOOF.

The company was founded in 1965. It has about 1,400 pet care centers across the U.S. and Puerto Rico, plus about 150 in Mexico and 2 in Chile through a joint venture. It also runs about 300 full-service veterinary hospitals and around 1,600 weekly Vetco clinics. No word on whether the cats mind the ticker.

This is a fun profile, not investment advice.
