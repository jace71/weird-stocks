Some tickers take a minute to decode. This one doesn't. The company is called Mammoth, so the stock trades as TUSK.

Mammoth Energy Services describes itself as a services company for the oil and natural gas, aviation and utility infrastructure industries. It also mines sand used in oil and gas wells. After Hurricane Maria, its Cobra subsidiary signed an emergency contract with Puerto Rico's power authority in October 2017 to repair the island's electrical grid.
