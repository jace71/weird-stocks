Not many companies on the stock market talk about death in their quarterly results. Matthews International does, because a big part of its business is memorialization: bronze and granite memorials, caskets, cremation products, and cremation and incineration equipment for cemeteries and funeral homes.

<h2>Embalming, too</h2>
In May 2025, Matthews bought The Dodge Company, which it calls a leading supplier of embalming chemicals and supplies in North America. Dodge had annual sales of about $43 million before the deal.

<h2>When fewer caskets are needed</h2>
In its third quarter of fiscal 2026, Matthews said sales of caskets and cemetery memorials were a headwind because of lower estimated U.S. casketed deaths, along with rising costs. The company also has an industrial technologies business and a brand solutions business, but the caskets get our attention.
