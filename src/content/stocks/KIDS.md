Most orthopedic device companies build implants for adults. OrthoPediatrics builds them for kids, so its ticker is KIDS.

The company, based in Warsaw, Indiana, says it is the only global medical device company focused exclusively on pediatric orthopedics. It makes implants, instruments and other products for children's trauma and deformity correction, scoliosis and sports medicine. It says it has helped more than 1,291,000 children since it started, counting those served by companies it acquired.
