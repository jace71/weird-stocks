Some companies hide what they do behind a clever ticker. Manhattan Bridge Capital just went with LOAN.

The New York company makes short-term, secured, non-bank loans, sometimes called "hard money" loans. Its borrowers are real estate investors who need cash to buy, fix up or improve properties in the New York metropolitan area, including New Jersey and Connecticut, and in Florida. Its portfolio is made up of first mortgage loans.

This is a fun profile, not investment advice.
