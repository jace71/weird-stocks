Muriel "Mickie" Siebert founded her brokerage in 1967. She was the first woman to own a seat on the New York Stock Exchange and the first woman to head one of its member firms.

<h2>An early discount broker</h2>
On May 1, 1975, after the federal government banned fixed brokerage commissions, Siebert turned her firm into a discount brokerage. Muriel Siebert & Co. still provides online and traditional brokerage services more than 55 years after it started.

Today it's the primary subsidiary of Siebert Financial Corp., which trades on Nasdaq as SIEB. The company has added other businesses over the years, including a media and entertainment company.
