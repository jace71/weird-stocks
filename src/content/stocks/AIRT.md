Air T is a small company with a grab bag of aviation businesses, and each one is pretty specific.

<h2>Little planes for FedEx</h2>
Two of its subsidiaries have worked with FedEx for more than 40 years. They operate and maintain small cargo planes, like Cessna Caravans, SkyCouriers and ATRs, that fly daily small-package routes in the eastern U.S., the upper Midwest and the Caribbean.

<h2>Deicing trucks</h2>
Another subsidiary, Global Ground Support in Olathe, Kansas, builds aircraft deicers and other airport equipment. Its customers include passenger and cargo airlines, airports and the U.S. Air Force. Deicing equipment made up about 84% of that unit's sales in the year ended March 31, 2026.

<h2>Old jets, parted out</h2>
Air T also leases aviation assets, sells used jet engine parts, and takes retired commercial aircraft apart to sell the pieces.
