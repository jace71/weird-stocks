Most companies pick a lane. Butler National Corporation, based in New Century, Kansas, picked two lanes that never cross. One half of the company modifies airplanes. The other half manages a casino. It trades on the OTCQX market under the ticker BUKS.

<h2>The airplane half</h2>
The Aerospace Products segment designs and installs structural modifications and electronic gear for Learjet, Challenger, Beechcraft King Air, and Cessna turboprop planes. Think camera ports cut into Cessna Caravans, rails and "Special Mission Pod" kits for the King Air, and a radome pod mounted under the belly of a Challenger to carry sensors.

Its Butler National-Tempe unit makes rugged electronic controls and cabling, including gun control units for the M134 minigun. In the quarter that ended July 31, 2026, this segment brought in $21.7 million, up 92% from a year earlier, and its backlog hit a record $51.1 million.

<h2>The casino half</h2>
The Professional Services segment manages Boot Hill Casino and Resort in Dodge City, Kansas. The company says it has about 500 slot machines, 15 table games, and a DraftKings branded sportsbook. That side brought in $9.0 million in the same quarter, up 3%.

So in one earnings release you get updates on special mission aircraft sensor packages and on slot machine spending. That's not something you see every day.

<h2>Growing, with an interim boss</h2>
Total revenue for the quarter rose 53% to $30.8 million, and the company kept buying back its own shares. Adam Sefchick is serving as interim CEO while the board searches for a permanent one.
