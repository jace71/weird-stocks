Bowlero had a pretty good ticker: BOWL. On December 12, 2024, it renamed itself Lucky Strike Entertainment and switched to a new ticker that's still a bowling joke: LUCK.

<h2>Lanes, arcades and water parks</h2>
The company has more than 360 locations across North America, with bowling, arcades, water parks and family entertainment centers. Some are traditional bowling alleys, and others are fancier spots with lounge seating and better food.

<h2>It owns the pros, too</h2>
Lucky Strike also owns the Professional Bowlers Association, the main sanctioning body for professional ten-pin bowling in the U.S. So the company runs the lanes and the pro tour.

On November 17, 2026, the company said founder and CEO Thomas F. Shannon had made a preliminary, non-binding proposal to take it private, buying the shares he doesn't already own. No deal has been agreed, and the stock still trades on the NYSE as LUCK.
